Resolume Timecode & MIDI

Two setups that break the same way — a countdown that should chase timecode, and a MIDI surface that should be moving the comp.

Timecode. Any countdown clip that should follow timecode needs the clip and the audio device both configured:

1

Point Arena at the Timecode Interface

Arena Preferences → Audio
  1. Set the input device to the Scarlett 2i2 — the standard device.
  2. Set an output device at the same time — Resolume wants both set, or the fields grey out.
2

Assign the SMPTE Input

Bottom of the same Audio pane
  1. Set SMPTE timecode input 1 to Input 1. Input 2 can be assigned as well.
3

Set the Clip to SMPTE Transport

The countdown clip's transport controls
  1. Change the clip's transport mode from Timeline to SMPTE.
If SMPTE isn't offered on the clip, the timecode device isn't configured — go back to step 1.

MIDI mapping. When a control needs to move — or the surface isn't doing what its labels say — this is the loop:

1

Open the MIDI Shortcuts Layer

Resolume · SHIFT M
  1. The whole interface lights up in tiles — every mappable parameter. Mapped ones show a CC number: that number is the control on the surface.
2

Assign or Re-Assign a Control

Still in the shortcuts layer
  1. Click the green tile for the parameter, then touch the control on the surface — the tile takes that control's number.
  2. Faders and knobs track their value; a button maps as on/off with no adjustment.
Example: to give a song a bump, find the Boing button on the Liquid Soul plug-in of the clip you'll be touching and map it to a spare control.
3

Set Limits and Mode on a Mapped Control

Click an already-mapped element for its extra controls
  1. Set a range for a value — e.g. clamp transition speed between 0.5 and 3 so the fader can never leave that window.
  2. Switch the control between absolute and relative if the pickup behaviour needs it.

Diagnose a Symptom

Open the symptom that matches what you're seeing. All Resolume so far — each checklist is worked top to bottom in order.

Dragged a visual in — nothing on the main screen
LOOK group → FLOW router → OUT RESO 1 layer 1 → Main Screen (SDI 3)
  1. Right group? Confirm the clip is in the LOOK group that FLOW is currently showing — trace the comp map top to bottom.
  2. Masters on OUT RESO 1 — visuals are layer 1, lyrics are layers 2–3; the group master and any of those layer masters can be sitting down.
  3. The surface can lie — the controller fader labelled Pixel Bars is an old carryover and actually rides OUT RESO 2, so a mislabelled fader left down can take out something you didn't touch.
  4. Layers 27–28 — the unlabelled special-effect layers at the top of OUT RESO 1 aren't predictable; check nothing is parked there over program.
On screen, but cropped, shifted, or carrying an effect you didn't add
FLOW router clip → OUT RESO layer 1
  1. Check the FLOW router clip for the active look — router clips take transforms and effects like any other clip, and leftover treatment hides there.
  2. Check width/height — 1920 × 1080 is standard. The comp gets copied and saved-over continuously, and a clip pasted from a special-event comp carries its old resolution edits with it.
NDI lyrics not keying cleanly over visuals
ProPresenter / Resolume NDI → OUT RESO 1 layers 2–3
  1. Check the NDI source's Alpha type — it must be Pre-multiplied, always. Receive format Auto is fine.
  2. Confirm which lyrics layer is up — Alpha Lyrics is layer 2, Lyrics Blend is layer 3 on OUT RESO 1.
Countdown ignores timecode — or SMPTE won't assign on the clip
Scarlett 2i2 → Arena Audio preferences → clip SMPTE transport
  1. SMPTE not offered on the clip? That's the tell that the timecode device isn't configured — set it up per Timecode & MIDI.
  2. Check the Audio pane — input device Scarlett 2i2, an output device also set (the fields grey out without one), SMPTE timecode input 1 on Input 1.
  3. Check the clip's transport — it must be on SMPTE, not Timeline.
MIDI surface does nothing — or not what its labels say
MIDI surface → CC map → comp
  1. Suspect the comp, not the surface — this usually follows a special event (Christmas, Easter) when a composition loaded from somewhere else arrives with foreign mappings, or none.
  2. Press SHIFT M — mapped parameters show their CC numbers. Re-assign anything wrong: click the tile, touch the control. Full loop on Timecode & MIDI.
  3. Control moves but behaves oddly? Click the mapped element and check its range limits and absolute/relative mode.
